FAQ

Questions clients ask us most.

How is pricing structured?

Every quote includes a full material and labour breakdown, shared before work begins — no hidden costs at any stage.

Which cities do you operate in?

We currently deliver projects across Mangaluru, Mysuru and Bengaluru, with site teams based in each city.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary with scope — a fixed milestone schedule is agreed and shared with you at the planning stage.

Do you handle renovations as well as new builds?

Yes — our team manages renovation, turnkey and interior projects alongside new residential and commercial builds.
