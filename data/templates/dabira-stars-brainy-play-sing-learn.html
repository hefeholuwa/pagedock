Rhyme Corner

Sing it, clap it, move it!

Open a rhyme to read the words and try the actions. Sing along with a grown-up or a friend.

Five bright stars up in the sky,
One fell down to say goodbye.
Four bright stars are twinkling still,
Count them slowly, count them, will you?

Hold up five fingers and fold one down with each verse.

Scrub, scrub, bubbles on my hands,
Rub them round like dancing bands.
Fingers, thumbs and in between,
Rinse them off and watch them gleam!

Mime washing and rubbing hands, then wiggle them dry.

Chug-a chug, the ABC train,
A is for apple at the station again.
B is for ball and C is for cat,
All aboard, let's sing like that!

Make train arms and chug round the room, one letter per step.

Good morning, sun, I'm up, I'm here,
Stretch up high and give a cheer!
Happy, sleepy, silly or shy,
Whatever I feel, I wave hi!

Stretch tall, then show a face for each feeling.
