A motion design tool that lives in your browser. Turn text, photos, backgrounds, and small animated elements into short videos. Pick a starting point, make it yours, and export.

Your first loop can happen in a few minutes. Start with a template, swap in your words, and adjust the pace. The presets already have the fiddly timing worked out.

Animated photo galleries, atmospheric backgrounds, counters, clocks, and short graphic loops. Add a shader or a little sound to pull the whole frame together.

Yes, there’s a free plan with the editor, core presets, fonts, and video export. You can try the interactive editor on this page without an account or a credit card.

The quick editor on this page exports four-second videos as WebM, or MP4 where your browser supports it. It automatically selects a supported format and downloads the finished loop.

The full editor supports your own fonts alongside the built-in collection. Just make sure your font license covers the way you plan to use and share your animation.

Yes. The Figma plugin brings your layout across with editable text. Start with the composition you already like, then add the movement it’s been missing.
