About
Lagos, Nigeria · Est. 2016

A lens on
real life.

I am Earth Xpression, a Lagos-based photographer with over eight years of experience crafting images that linger. My work spans editorial, commercial, portrait, and documentary photography — always in pursuit of the frame that feels honest.

My visual language is rooted in the textures of West Africa — the quality of light at dusk in Lagos, the energy of street markets, the quiet intimacy of a portrait session. I bring the same deliberate eye to every project, whether it's a fashion spread or a brand campaign.

Clients include agencies, editorial publications, NGOs, and private collectors across Nigeria, the UK, and the United States.
